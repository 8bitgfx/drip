# Adapt (Drip)

Applies when the user asks Drip to become good at a niche. Call `drip_guide adapt` for the full rules; it records the read. (Not in your tools? ToolSearch `select:mcp__plugin_drip_drip__drip_guide`.)

- `pack_list` first: an existing pack means a refresh, not a new pack.
- Research order: hard constraints and official standards, references, shipped products, the 10-20 artists at the top end, public skills.
- Every claim has a source in `sources.md`; a standard you couldn't confirm is marked "verify".
- Defaults are testable (a number, a count, a named element); at least 70% of rules fail the swap test.
