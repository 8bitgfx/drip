# References (Drip)

Step 2 of the full loop; the fast loop looks with `refs_site` and `refs_gallery`. Call `drip_guide refs` for the full rules; it records the read. (Not in your tools? ToolSearch `select:mcp__plugin_drip_drip__drip_guide`.)

- Search from the brief, from 3+ angles, and go past page 1.
- View the original full size; never study from a thumbnail.
- Study with values (a size, ratio, hex, count or named type class); "clean" and "modern" are rejected.
- Take one move per reference, never a whole composition; never copy illustration, photography, logos, copy or a signature effect.
