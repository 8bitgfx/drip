# Patterns (Drip)

Applies when something is to be built like a reference. Call `drip_guide patterns` for the full rules; it records the read. (Not in your tools? ToolSearch `select:mcp__plugin_drip_drip__drip_guide`.)

- Measure, don't eyeball: `refs_extract` or `pattern_study` first; a screenshot-only reference is labelled inferred.
- Build your own version in your tokens on an accessible primitive; never paste the reference's markup, copy, images or brand.
- Real behaviour, not a picture of it: states by real input, timings and easing from the spec.
- `pattern_save` records provenance; status stays `proposed` until the user accepts it.
