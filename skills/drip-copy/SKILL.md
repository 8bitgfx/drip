# Copy (Drip)

Applies to every word on a page. Call `drip_guide copy` for the full rules; it records the read. (Not in your tools? ToolSearch `select:mcp__plugin_drip_drip__drip_guide`.)

- The headline says what the product is and who it is for, in 8 words or fewer, and fails the swap test.
- Buttons are a verb plus an object; never "Submit", "Learn more" or a lone "Get started".
- Never invent numbers, customer counts, logos, quotes, ratings or awards.
- No eyebrow labels, no 01/02/03 unless the order is real; errors say what happened and what to do next.
