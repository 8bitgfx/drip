# Landing pages (Drip)

Applies to any page whose job is to make a stranger understand an offer and act. Call `drip_guide landing` for the full rules; it records the read. (Not in your tools? ToolSearch `select:mcp__plugin_drip_drip__drip_guide`.)

- The h1 names what is sold and for whom in 2 to 8 plain words; the first screen holds one hard fact.
- The hero's picture is the product, place or work itself; software shows its real UI big, once.
- Proof comes only from real material; never invent testimonials, stats, logos, ratings or user counts.
- The main action has one wording everywhere; no dark patterns.
