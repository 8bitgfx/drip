# Icons (Drip)

Applies to every icon on a page. Call `drip_guide icons` for the full rules; it records the read. (Not in your tools? ToolSearch `select:mcp__plugin_drip_drip__drip_guide`.)

- One icon family per project, chosen once and written in `DESIGN.md`; brand marks are the only second source.
- Never emoji, Unicode symbols or drawn icons.
- Sizes on the family's grid (16, 20, 24); stroke follows the text beside it; icons are `currentColor`.
- Every icon-only control has an accessible name; an icon must carry meaning or be the control.
