# Product shots (Drip)

Applies to every faux or mocked UI picture. Call `drip_guide productshots` for the full rules; it records the read. (Not in your tools? ToolSearch `select:mcp__plugin_drip_drip__drip_guide`.)

- Write the plan before code: where it sits, the one focal object, the one thing that happens, the payoff, what is left out.
- It reads as a real product, simplified: full structure in quiet greys, real words only on the 3 to 7 strings the story is about.
- One hero object; it fills at least two thirds of the stage and runs 10 to 40% off an edge.
- Real chrome and real logos only; faux UI is a picture: `role="img"` with an `aria-label`.
