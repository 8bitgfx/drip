# App UI (Drip)

Applies to screens people work in, not to marketing pages. Call `drip_guide app` for the full rules; it records the read. (Not in your tools? ToolSearch `select:mcp__plugin_drip_drip__drip_guide`.)

- Someone fluent in the category finds everything where they expect it: standard navigation, standard controls.
- Every data region shows loading, empty, error and full states.
- Accent on action, selection and state only.
- Tables before cards for collections; state lives in the URL.
