# Studio (Drip)

Applies to original artwork, shaders, mockups and renders. Call `drip_guide studio` for the full rules; it records the read. (Not in your tools? ToolSearch `select:mcp__plugin_drip_drip__drip_guide`.)

- Artwork belongs to the direction: pass the chosen palette (first colour = background) and mood; a default-palette render is a draft.
- Artwork and shaders are material, not the idea: keep the zone behind text calm and check contrast on the final page.
- One signature visual per page; mockups show the user's real screenshot or approved artwork.
- Keep the spec file and re-render instead of starting over; a render is a candidate, so look at it.
