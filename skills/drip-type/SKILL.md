# Typography (Drip)

Applies to every text style on a page. Call `drip_guide type` for the full rules; it records the read. (Not in your tools? ToolSearch `select:mcp__plugin_drip_drip__drip_guide`.)

- One family, or two with clearly different jobs; a third only for code or figures. Do not default to Inter, Roboto, Open Sans, Poppins, DM Sans or Space Grotesk.
- A real scale: 5-7 sizes per surface; reading text 16px or more; nothing under 12px.
- Body line-height 1.5-1.65, headings 1.05-1.25; measure 45-75 characters.
- Three weights at most; `tabular-nums` on changing numbers; no tracked all-caps eyebrow, no gradient text.
