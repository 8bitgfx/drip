# Direction (Drip)

Full-loop step after the board; the fast loop takes its direction from `layout_deal` and the references. Call `drip_guide direction` for the full rules; it records the read. (Not in your tools? ToolSearch `select:mcp__plugin_drip_drip__drip_guide`.)

- One idea per surface, written as a thesis: what it leads with, and the category default it refuses.
- Light or dark comes from the scene sentence (who, where, under what light), never from habit.
- One genre leads; no hybrids. Check the built CSS against its `breaks_if` list.
- The chosen direction is locked: sharpen it, never swap its idea, palette or type voice without asking.
