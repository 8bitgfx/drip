# Ambition: never settle for the safe default

Drip's default ambition is **bold**. "Make a portfolio for Claude" does not mean "make a tasteful, corporate
page". It means make the portfolio people would screenshot. Only go plain, quiet or corporate when the user
asks for it ("simple", "conservative", "match our existing corporate site"), or when the surface is a tool
people work in (then ambition shows as precision, not spectacle).

## The test, before you show anything
1. **Screenshot test.** Would someone screenshot this and send it to a friend? If not, it isn't done.
2. **Competitor swap.** Put a competitor's name on it. Still works? Then it has no idea.
3. **Default check.** Could an AI produce this from the category name alone? Then it's the default.
4. **One sentence.** Can you state the concept in one sentence that couldn't describe anything else?
5. **One risk.** Name the decision a cautious designer wouldn't make. No risk, no conviction.

## The defaults Drip refuses (unless the brief literally asks)
| Default | Why it's dead | Go instead |
|---|---|---|
| Cream/beige paper + terracotta/rust accent + polite serif ("tasteful AI") | It's what every model makes when asked for "warm" or "premium" | A palette from the subject's own world; a type voice with a point of view |
| Centred hero, two buttons, three equal icon cards | The template skeleton | Structure from the content's own form: an index, a timeline, a live demo, a single huge statement |
| Near-black + one neon + glow | The "AI startup" costume | Dark only if the scene is dark; colour from the subject |
| Purple/indigo/pink gradients, glass cards, blobs, sparkles | Generated-by-default tells | Real material: the product, the work, photography, type |
| Inter/Geist/system font as the voice | No voice | A display face chosen like an object from the subject's world |
| "Clean, modern, professional" | Decides nothing | Three adjectives that rule things out |

## How to be bold without being bad
- **Spend boldness in one place**: one signature move that owns the page, everything else disciplined.
- **Commit every atom**: buttons, nav, footer, empty states all speak the direction's language.
- **Scale up**: when in doubt, make the headline bigger, the colour field larger, the image fuller.
- **Borrow ambition, never clothes**: a portfolio can take the confidence of a great museum or label site, but a page dressed as a poster, a book cover or a record sleeve is a picture of a world, not its website. References are shipped sites of the same kind (`refs_site`).
- **Directions must differ in idea, not style**: at least one direction crosses the subject with a genre from another world ("unexpected pairing"), with one line on why it beats the safe one.
- **Name it like a movement**: each direction gets a 1-2 word name and a 3-sentence manifesto before anything is drawn.

## The critic enforces this
The drip:critic subagent caps Idea and Conviction at 4/10 for any default above, and rejects directions that are
"the same idea rendered twice". It never grades down committed loudness or density; it grades down hedging.
