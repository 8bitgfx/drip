# Changelog

What each release changed and why (newest first). Open problems are in [docs/NEXT.md](docs/NEXT.md); older history is in the git log.

## 0.10.6: close-ups of a film
- **Close-ups**: `motion_study({ source, from, to })` watches one stretch densely (about 36 frames over up to 30 s, 3 a second for a 12-second stretch) with the change frame to frame and where it peaks, and asks for the transition read from the frames: one element followed across them, easing from the spacing. The full study now names its busiest stretches with the close-up call ready (on the Gemini 3 film: 0:40-0:55, where the input box becomes the whole UI, which the full sheet skips). `motion_storyboard` warns when no moves reference longer than 15 s was watched up close.

## 0.10.5: narration after the cut is approved, launch films, any post studied
- **No paid voice before the picture is final**: `motion_voice` speak and audition (ElevenLabs, paid per character) need a rendered video of the current version, a clean frame sweep of it, and the user's own words approving the cut (`cutApproved`, checked against the chat by the claims hook); the approval holds for that version only. Before that everything is free: casting from preview links, and `speak` with `dryRun` now estimates each line's start and end so the animatic is timed to the script. Generated music's `userAsked` is checked against the chat too.
- **Any post can be studied**: `motion_study` plays public YouTube, X, Vimeo, Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn posts (yt-dlp's official release, fetched once into `~/.drip/vendor/bin`, takes a 720p MP4 with no account; Vimeo through its player URL). Pages with no playable video fall back to it too. A brand's launch film can now be studied from its own post.
- **Launch films first**: for a launch, product, AI, SaaS or app brief, `motion_refs` leads with 29 official launch and brand films of AI, SaaS and developer-tool brands (`content/data/launch-films.json`: Cursor, Vercel v0, Linear, Raycast, Notion, Gemini, Runway, Ramp…, each checked to play and looked at; mostly-on-camera ones are marked for story, not moves). The guides send the agent to WebSearch the closest real launches the list lacks.
- **On-topic references**: a master's piece shows only when its title fits the subject (synonyms count: "launch" finds "Introducing…", "ai" finds GPT and agents), long queries never fall back to lone words, tiles whose poster didn't load are dropped, and open search takes at most two pieces per maker. A reference's sparse frames are reported as what it does, not as something to fix.
- **References come with their words**: `motion_refs` no longer returns one numbered poster sheet. Each film is its own entry: title, what its maker wrote in the post (Dribbble shot descriptions; Kisto pieces' Vimeo or YouTube posts; launch films' own descriptions, stored), length, aspect, date, views, likes, palette and tags, then one frame of it. Cached, and limited to 40 s; anything not back in time is shown without it.
- **Music is text**: `motion_music` analyse gives the track's energy bar by bar as text (8 bars a line, with sections, builds, drops and the storyboard's beats marked) instead of a chart image.
- **The Stop hook stops nagging a waiting agent**: when the agent's last message asks the user something (a plan to approve, a direction to pick), the turn ends without re-prompting; films are spoken of as films (frame check, not fit check).

## 0.10.4: /drip:settings reads as a result, not an error
- The settings shortcut ends the prompt with continue: false and a plain message (the link), instead of decision "block", which the desktop app showed as a red "A hook blocked your prompt" card. The typed command is left out of the stop message.

## 0.10.3: SnapCN pieces in films
- **SnapCN pieces in films**: `motion_piece` searches SnapCN's ~145 video pieces and adds one to a film: its MIT source goes into `<film>/pieces/src`, bundled (esbuild, installed by the agent) into `pieces/<name>.js` with a clean-room Remotion stand-in driven by the film's clock, so pieces seek exactly and every renderer plays them (mount with `DripPieces.mount`). `motion_storyboard` lists SnapCN pieces that may fit each beat, and film tasks check SnapCN and the visuals library before hand-building. Tested on text-reveal, dot-bloom, count-grid and logo-assemble. Seeks now wait for images and fonts (the same time always gives the same picture); `DripVisuals.drive` adds to what the timeline drives instead of replacing it; the light field always sits behind the stage; a renderer that mismatches Chrome is skipped for that film only.

## 0.10.2: Amicro and sensory-ui
- **Amicro** (amicro.dev, MIT): 176 Motion + Tailwind micro-interactions (morphing icon buttons, magnetic and glare buttons, card spreads, 3D carousels, loaders, text reveals, mono and dither charts) in the catalogue, searchable with components_find. amicro.dev answers scripts with a Vercel bot check, so its registry is read from the GitHub repo.
- **sensory-ui** (Shadcn Labs, MIT): sound for shadcn/ui, 24 drop-in components with a `sound` prop, synthesized with Web Audio. taste.md says when a page gets sound (opt-in, small actions small sounds, never on hover or scroll).

## 0.10.1: /drip:settings without the model, and fixes from a drip-saas run
- The prompt hook catches `/drip:settings`, starts the settings page in its own background process (`drip settings --ttl 60`, which stops by itself after an hour), opens the browser and stops the prompt with the link. No model call, no tokens. `commands/settings.md` stays as the fallback for hosts without the hook.
- **page_stitch is safe to run twice**: a section App.tsx already imports or renders outside Drip's markers is left there, and a second import of one name is removed (it blanked the page with no error).
- **A blank page says so**: the fit check collects the page's uncaught errors and console errors; a page with no words and no pictures is one blocker, "the page rendered blank", with those errors. Other console errors are a warning.
- **pieces_install tidies what registry items leave**: new empty stub files are removed, a file the install emptied gets its content back, code written outside `src/` (a stray `components/` at the page root) moves under `src/` (or is removed when it's an identical copy), and empty folders go. `section:` records a piece swap for that section, so replacing a broken piece needs no new `build_prepare`.
- **Leftover tasks stay their session's**: a session's server no longer takes over a task another session left open (it made a new session's Stop hook demand checks for work it never did). Continuing one is explicit: `drip_status({ resume: true })`. A skipped task no longer shows as "active" in every prompt, nor in the session-start pointer.
- **Fit-check false alarms**: screen-reader-only text (1px clipped boxes) is never measured as "cut off"; smooth scrolling (CSS `scroll-behavior`, Lenis, Locomotive) is turned off in the measuring tab, so nothing is measured mid-scroll (a nav "covering" an h1 at a negative top).
- **Canvases that draw nothing**: every canvas below the first screen is scrolled into view and looked at once; one that stays a single flat colour (a WebGL piece that failed in Chrome, solid black) is a blocker.
- **Type scale**: pieces' own Tailwind sizes (`text-sm`, `text-[15px]`) are listed in the piece review; more than 9 text sizes on a page is "type off the scale" and blocks in the fast loop too, with the stray sizes named.
- **Dead links**: 6+ links to `href="#"` (a footer of placeholders) block; 3-5 warn, with what to do.
- **Friendlier refusals**: a vague `refs_study` shows a complete accepted study and the site's own measured numbers; `images_search` rounds a small `limit` up to 4 instead of failing, and filters 30 more watermarked stock sites; a refused `drip_skip` shows the user's latest message to quote.

## 0.10.0: commands by craft, and Drip Motion
- **Fixes from the first real film** (the Drip trailer):
  - `drip_start({ project })` keeps a task's work and Drip state in its own folder (`~/.drip/workspaces.json`, followed by the server and every hook, cleared by `drip_end`), so a film made from another repo's session never writes `.drip` there.
  - A film's critic is one review, not a gate: a REVISE no longer holds every turn open or blocks `drip_end`; its open points go to the user in the hand-off. Only a clean frame sweep on the current files holds a turn.
  - The camera is the camera: `motion_trace` fits one shared move per frame (or uses the group marked `data-camera`) and measures each element's own motion inside it; rigid groups count as one move; invisible motion is ignored. `motion_study` re-checks every candidate cut frame by frame and reports fast pushes as moves, not cuts.
  - The clipped-text check skips text nobody can see (opacity down the tree, clip-paths, masks, cropping containers).
  - The critic gets three full-size frames for judging sizes, treats in-betweens as transitions, and judges the film rather than the plan.
  - Sound cues count pixel change (an SVG path changing shape) as movement; music files get readable names and audio extensions; ShareAlike tracks rank last (syncing music makes the film an adaptation).
  - Film support files (fonts.css, data scripts) are free of the composition hold; @font-face declarations don't count as font families.
- **Commands**: `/drip:design` is now `/drip:ui` (pages, screens, components, polish); new `/drip:logo` (logos, icons, vector marks) and `/drip:motion` (films). `/drip:adapt` takes a niche, a style or a workflow; `/drip:critic` judges films and videos too.
- **Drip Motion** (`modules/motion`, skill `drip-video`, `drip_start` mode `motion`): motion-design films made in code (HTML + GSAP/CSS/canvas/three.js) and rendered locally by seeking every frame. Its own loop and gates: a studied motion reference, then a checked storyboard (`motion_storyboard`: concept, hook, motif, beats, references; slop copy is an error), before composition files; a clean `motion_frames` sweep on the current version before a turn ends; the critic once (`critic_prepare` kind `motion`) before `drip_end`. Page and logo gates don't apply, but their tools (pieces, mockups, logo_sheet) are open to a film.
- **Seeing motion, not stills**: `motion_frames` (timestamped sheets, transition strips, dense ranges; page errors, blank frames, clipped text, flicker), `motion_trace` (every element's position, size and opacity per frame from the page: a dopesheet against the beats, onion skins of each beat's spacing and arcs, flags for dead stops, linear moves, hitches, everything starting at once, one duration for all, mostly fades). The critic gets all three.
- **References, masters first**: `motion_refs` starts from `content/data/motion-masters.json` (26 studios and designers vetted by watching their work: TimeFrame, ManvsMachine, Tendril, Ordinary Folk, Oddfellows…), picked by the kind of film asked for; then Dribbble's animation category with a quality floor (video, no boosts, 40+ likes, no UI showcases) and Kisto (no AI clips, tutorials or rejected makers). 24fps.dev (indie films in code) is opt-in, for technique. A Dribbble shot URL plays its full video in `motion_study`. Earlier: plain keyword search; `motion_study` plays any video (blob-loaded, so hosts without byte ranges seek) and measures cuts, shot length and rhythm. Guides: `video/studios` (18 films from 10 studios, measured), `video/house-style` (TimeFrame's work, measured: the default look and pacing), `video/components` (SnapCN, added to the catalogue).
- **Sound, found first**: `motion_music` (licensed tracks via Openverse/Jamendo; analysis in the browser: BPM, beat grid, bars, sections, builds, drops, a chart, storyboard-to-bar offsets; Eleven Music generation only on the user's word), `motion_sound` (CC0 effects from sfx.akalan.dev incl. SFX Kit, foleykit, Kenney, Openverse; calm UI sounds by default, placed by each sound's sync point; whooshes and density flagged), `motion_voice` (ElevenLabs narration: casting from the user's voices and the Voice Library, auditions, chained recording with word timings, pace and cost checked first). `motion_export` mixes it all with the music ducked under the voice. The ElevenLabs key lives on the settings page.
- **Renderers, set up by the agent**: the first `/drip:motion` installs Kinetograph (>=0.4.0-rc.1, Apache-2.0: GPU, no browser), FrameVelo (`--no-hyperframes`), HyperFrames, and full ffmpeg/ffprobe builds (Kinetograph's own FFmpeg can't mix audio) into `~/.drip/vendor` in the background, test-renders a 2 s 720p clip with each (and Drip's own capture), keeps the fastest that works in `~/.drip/motion-renderer.json` and never asks again. `motion_export` (engine `auto`) falls back in measured order and mixes the sound on afterwards. Drafts, ranges and alpha use Drip's capture.
- **Renders must match Chrome**: `motion_export` compares four frames of every external render (Kinetograph, FrameVelo, HyperFrames) with Chrome's frames of the composition, region by region; a mismatch falls back to the next renderer, and two mismatches retire a renderer on that machine. FrameVelo is given the composition file (it wanted index.html).
- **Music made to cut to**: `motion_music` search ranks for motion-design fit (`feel: "motion"` by default: rhythmic clean electronic, synth-pop, nu-disco, future garage, chillhop and funky beats at a 100-125 BPM pulse with crisp hits; `energetic` for big launches; `ambient` only for slow atmospheric pieces; drones, solo piano, orchestral, jazz and lounge rank down), turns product words into music searches, and rules out rock, metal, club and vocal tracks.
- **UI principles in films**: `video/ui` brings Drip's UI bar into films (real content, one system, states, sizes for video, UI timings vs film timings); product films load it at start, UI beats can't be storyboarded without it, and the critic judges the UI on screen.
- **Kit**: `drip_kit` template `motion`: a HyperFrames-style composition (`data-composition-id` root, `window.__timelines["main"]`, plus `window.__drip.seek`) so every renderer plays the same file; one paused timeline, seeded `rand()`, a closed-form `spring()` ease and a scrubbing preview. Narration word timings come as `sound/voice-words.js` (compositions never fetch).

- Builders keep the idea that made a piece the pick and review the rest against the page: colours, radius, borders, shadows, hover and press effects, spacing, fonts, motion timing. ## 0.9.7: no placeholders, no pills, visuals that show at rest
- **No placeholders, ever.** Prices, plans, product data and sample outputs are written (realistic, listed as assumptions at hand-off); customers, quotes and user counts that only the world can say are left out; pictures are made or found, never empty slots. taste.md, the playbook, the builder brief and the intake say so; the page check now also blocks blank prices (`$XX`, `$—`, `$__`) and `TBD`.
- **Pieces restyled or not done**: a used piece whose files still carry 4+ demo colour classes (zinc, slate, gray, neutral, blue, indigo, violet...) is a blocker (`pieces-check.mjs`).
- **Pills, tags and badges are out**: a pill above the h1, or 3+ pill-shaped labels anywhere, blocks the page check.
- **Simplicity and abstract visuals**: taste.md prefers few striking visuals (a dotted or ASCII globe spinning, particle fields, constellations, waveforms) over busy tech demos; bentos are odd-sized grids where each cell is a visual (equal-cell grids are flagged).
- **Striking at rest, cooler on interaction**: a big canvas, video or data-visual in the first screen that is one flat colour on the untouched shot blocks (99.5%+ flat, text ignored); 98.5-99.5% warns as faint.

## 0.9.0: cost
The anime run's 28.5M tokens were the director's context (83k at the start, 411k at the end) re-read on each of 116 calls. Cost = calls x context, so 0.9.0 cuts both:
- **Briefs in files** (`core/briefs.mjs`): `build_prepare` and `critic_prepare` write each brief to `.drip/briefs/` and return a two-line launch call. Before, a builder brief sat in the director's context twice (tool result, then the Agent call).
- **Fewer pictures, smaller**: fast loop fit checks show one small sheet per size the first time the page is seen (`fitcheck.seenAt`), numbers only after that (still recorded as the check); `refs_site` gives one 960px sheet per site instead of every screen; piece previews are 960px wide.
- **One fix list**: after 6 edits by the director since the last page check (`directorEdits`, reset by fitcheck_shot and build_prepare), the write gate asks for one list: a SendMessage to the builder that wrote the section, or one MultiEdit.
- **No scouts or checkers in the fast loop**; helpers only for long mechanical jobs; installs and downloads are tools.
- **Critic optional in the fast loop** (`gates.fastCritic`, default false): offered in the hand-off, not required by `drip_end`. A REVISE it gave still needs the user's choice.
- **Retries inside tools**: `pieces_install` retries registry timeouts twice; `fitcheck_shot` retries once when the page reloads under it.
- **All gaps at once**: `drip_status`'s NEXT lists everything missing, not the first item.
