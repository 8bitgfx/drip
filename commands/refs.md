Find design references for: $ARGUMENTS

Call `drip_guide` with topic `refs` first and follow it. Run at least 3 different searches (`refs_search`, then fan out with `refs_related`, `refs_tag`, `refs_designer`), scan the contact sheets, and open the 8-15 strongest with `refs_view`. For each, write what exactly is worth taking from it. Finish with a short summary of patterns you saw across the references, and offer to put them on a moodboard.
